Pumpkin voice for in-app copy

Rules for every word on a screen, notification or email inside the Pumpkin app: buttons, labels, empty states, errors, push notifications, onboarding, settings and consent. Source of truth: the Pumpkin Brand Voice doc (tab "AI replies and app copy"). Change both together.

The five checks

Before any string ships, it passes all five.

Does it say the next step? What happens now, and who does it.
Would a tired friend at the kitchen table get it on the first read? One idea per sentence, everyday words.
Is it specific? A time, a name, an amount or a date beats an adjective.
Does it say who decides? Anything clinical names the nurse or doctor.
Does it assume anything about who they are? Family, gender, partner, pregnancy plans, why someone donated. If yes, rewrite it.
Mechanics
Sentence case for everything, including buttons and tabs.
No emoji, no exclamation points, no em dashes.
Contractions are fine and preferred.
Numerals for doses, dates, money and times: "8:00 pm," "$6,133," "Day 6." Units every time: "612 pg/mL."
Times in the patient's time zone, with "am" and "pm" in lowercase.
Names: "Pumpkin" in copy. In a clinic-branded app the clinic is the name people see, with "Powered by Pumpkin" in the background. "Kinpath" never appears in the product.
Sample or demo content is always labeled "Sample data."
Length
Element	Limit	Example
Button	1 to 3 words, a verb	"Log dose," "Message nurse," "Add your person"
Screen title	Up to 4 words	"Tonight's meds"
Notification title	Up to 6 words	"Your 8:00 pm injections"
Notification body	Up to 15 words	"3 injections tonight. Tap to see each one."
Empty state	1 line plus a button	"No results yet. They'll show up here when your clinic sends them."
Error	1 to 2 sentences	"That didn't save. Check your connection and try again."
Buttons and links

A verb and the outcome. Never "Learn more," "Submit," "OK" or "Click here" on its own.

Instead of	Write
Submit	Send to your nurse
Learn more	What this measures
OK	Got it
Continue	Next: your meds
Delete	Delete this entry
Empty states

Say what will appear and when. Offer one action if there is one.

"No messages yet. When you message your nurse, the thread lives here."
"Nothing logged today. Your 8:00 pm dose will show here once you log it."
Errors

Say what happened, then what to do. Never blame them, never use codes on their own, never "Oops."

"We couldn't send that. Your message is saved. Try again when you're back online."
"That photo is too large. Try one under 10 MB."
For anything clinical that fails: "This didn't reach your nurse. If it's urgent, call {nurse_line}."
Notifications
Lead with the time or the task: "8:00 pm: 3 injections."
Never put results, diagnoses, medication names or pregnancy status on the lock screen. Say "You have a new result from your clinic," not the result.
No notifications for marketing or billing during the two-week wait or after a negative result or loss.
Quiet mode: after a negative result or loss, offer it, don't force it. "Want us to keep things quiet for a while? Only your clinic's messages will come through."
The AI in the interface
Every AI answer carries a label. Clinic mode: "Answered from {clinic_name}'s protocol" or "Drafted with Pumpkin, reviewed by {nurse_name}." On their own: "Answered by Pumpkin's AI."
"Urgent? Call the nurse line" is always visible in any screen where they can ask a question.
The composer hint names the limit: "Ask about your meds, schedule or costs. Urgent symptoms go straight to a person."
Never use "AI nurse," "medical advice" or "24/7 care."
Results screens
Show the number, the unit and what it measures, in everyday words first.
Clinic mode: show the clinic's expected range only if the clinic supplied it, labeled "{clinic_name}'s expected range."
On their own: no range, no color coding, no arrows, no "normal." Add "Your care team reads this with your other results."
Sharing and consent

Say exactly who sees what, in the moment they choose.

"Your person can see your schedule and meds. They can't see your results or messages."
"Your nurse will see what you log here."
Consent screens: what we collect, who sees it, how to change it later. One idea per line. No legal language in the main text. Link to the full policy.
Onboarding
Ask, don't assume. "Who's on this with you?" with options that include "Just me for now."
Let people skip anything optional, and say so: "You can add this later."
Pronouns and names they give are used everywhere, including notifications to their person.
Hard moments

After a negative result or loss, and during the two-week wait:

No exclamation points, no celebrations, no streaks, no "Great job logging."
No billing reminders or upsells.
No pregnancy dates, due dates or baby imagery.
Fewer words. "We're so sorry. You don't have to decide anything today."
Words

Use: your nurse decides, the next step, your record, your clinic's range, one place, your person (once they've named one), a cycle that didn't work.

Avoid: journey, every step of the way, made simple, seamless, don't worry, stress-free, easy, failed cycle, husband, wife, mom-to-be, infertile, normal (about results), pumpkin puns.
